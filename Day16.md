# 📊 Day 16 – Claude Custom Skill: Stock Fundamental Research

## 🎯 Objective

The objective of Day 16 was to create a reusable **Claude Custom Skill** for stock fundamental research. Instead of entering the same prompt repeatedly, the skill automates structured company analysis using publicly available financial data and presents investor-friendly reports.

---

# 🛠️ Custom Skill Details

**Skill Name:** `stock-fundamental-research`

### Description

Analyze Indian and global listed companies using fundamentals, financial statements, business quality, competitive advantages, valuation, risks, and growth prospects. Generate evidence-based research reports and investor-friendly summaries without providing buy, sell, or hold recommendations.

---

# 📌 Task Completed

* Created a reusable Custom Skill in Claude.
* Added detailed research instructions.
* Configured the skill for multiple analysis modes (Quick Take, Deep Dive, Compare, Pros & Cons, Portfolio Fit).
* Tested the skill by comparing **TCS** and **Infosys**.
* Generated a structured fundamental comparison report.
* Reviewed valuation metrics, ownership trends, profitability, financial health, and risk factors.
* Verified how the Custom Skill can be reused without re-entering prompts.

---

# 📈 Stock Comparison

## Companies Compared

* Tata Consultancy Services (TCS)
* Infosys Ltd.

---

# 🔍 Key Financial Metrics

| Metric            |          TCS |      Infosys |
| ----------------- | -----------: | -----------: |
| CMP               |       ₹1,983 |       ₹1,047 |
| Market Cap        | ₹7,16,423 Cr | ₹4,24,910 Cr |
| Stock P/E         |         13.7 |         14.1 |
| Dividend Yield    |        3.23% |        4.58% |
| ROE               |        51.8% |        31.9% |
| ROCE              |        63.0% |        40.0% |
| Operating Margin  |          27% |          24% |
| Revenue CAGR (5Y) |          10% |          12% |

---

# ✅ Where TCS Leads

* Larger market capitalization and business scale.
* Higher operating margins.
* Excellent ROE and ROCE.
* Strong Tata Group promoter backing.
* Industry-leading capital efficiency.

---

# ✅ Where Infosys Leads

* Higher dividend yield.
* Faster long-term revenue growth.
* Better recent earnings momentum.
* Attractive valuation at similar P/E levels.

---

# ⚠️ Common Risk Factors

* Dependence on global IT spending.
* Exposure to BFSI sector demand.
* Currency exchange fluctuations.
* AI-driven disruption in traditional IT services.
* Wage inflation and margin pressure.
* Global macroeconomic uncertainty.

---

# 📊 Overall Analysis

Both companies demonstrate:

* Strong balance sheets.
* Low financial leverage.
* High profitability.
* Consistent cash generation.
* Well-established global businesses.

The comparison highlighted that **TCS emphasizes scale, profitability, and operational efficiency**, while **Infosys offers stronger dividend income and comparatively faster growth**.

The report intentionally avoided investment recommendations and focused only on evidence-based fundamental analysis.
