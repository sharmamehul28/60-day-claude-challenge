# 🚚 Day 29 – Operation Lifeline: Supply Chain Crisis Lab

## 📌 Project Overview

On Day 29 of the ABTalks 60 Days AI Challenge, I built **Operation Lifeline: Supply Chain Crisis Lab** using Claude AI.

This interactive React-based business simulation allows users to experience real-world supply chain crisis management. Players take on the role of a business leader, responding to operational disruptions, negotiating with suppliers, making executive decisions, and investing in AI-powered solutions to improve resilience and business performance.

---

# 🎯 Objective

- Simulate real-world supply chain crises
- Practice executive decision-making
- Improve negotiation skills
- Understand AI applications in supply chain management
- Learn business resilience strategies
- Build an interactive React application

---

# 🚀 Core Features

## 🏢 Random Company Generator

Every playthrough creates a unique fictional company including:

- Industry
- Revenue
- Factories
- Warehouses
- Suppliers
- Inventory Days
- Lead Time
- Countries of Operation

This ensures every simulation feels different.

---

## ⚠️ Supply Chain Crisis Simulation

A random crisis is generated such as:

- Factory Fire
- Supplier Bankruptcy
- Port Strike
- Cyberattack
- Flood
- Raw Material Shortage
- Political Conflict
- Shipping Delay

Each crisis includes urgency level and business impact.

---

## 🛡 War Room Decision Making

Players choose **three response actions** from six available strategies.

Business metrics update dynamically:

- Cost
- Inventory
- Profit
- Delivery Speed
- Customer Satisfaction

Animated progress bars visualize the impact of every decision.

---

## 🤝 Supplier Negotiation

A four-round negotiation challenges players to balance:

- Trust
- Pricing
- Lead Time

Each decision contributes to the final negotiation score.

---

## 👨‍💼 CEO Boardroom

Players answer leadership-focused multiple-choice questions to evaluate executive decision-making skills.

---

## 🤖 AI Strategy Planning

Choose two AI investments from:

- Demand Forecasting
- Inventory Optimization
- Supplier Risk Monitoring
- Warehouse Vision
- Procurement Copilot

The simulator estimates the business impact of each investment.

---

## 📊 Executive Dashboard

The final report includes:

- Overall Crisis Score
- Leadership Score
- Negotiation Score
- Resilience
- Cost Control
- Risk Management
- Customer Satisfaction

Additional insights:

- Biggest Mistake
- Best Decision
- Expert Recommendation
- Lessons Learned

---
