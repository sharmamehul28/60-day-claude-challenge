# 🧭 Day 37 – Task Compass

## 📌 Overview

Today, I built **Task Compass**, an interactive workplace management simulation designed to demonstrate how work flows through a real Tech Company.

The application focuses on ownership, delegation, collaboration, and workflow thinking through engaging drag-and-drop activities and realistic workplace scenarios.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

Build a strategy-style learning experience that teaches organizational workflows, responsibility, and teamwork instead of testing job titles.

---

## ✨ Features

- 💼 Tech Company Workplace Simulation
- 👨‍💻 8 Interactive Role Cards
- 🎯 Stage 1 – Who Owns This?
- 🔄 Stage 2 – Task Routing
- 🤝 Stage 3 – Collaboration Challenge
- 🎮 Drag & Drop Gameplay
- 📊 Organizational Thinking Dashboard
- 🎉 Completion Celebration with Confetti
- 📈 Progress Tracking
- 💬 Detailed Explanations after Every Challenge
- 🌙 Premium Dark Glassmorphism UI
- 📱 Responsive Design
- 💻 Fully Offline Single HTML Application

---

## 📚 Key Learnings

- Ownership does not mean working alone.
- Delegation helps organizations work efficiently.
- Complex workplace challenges require cross-functional collaboration.
- Effective workflow improves communication between teams.
- Understanding organizational structure leads to better decision-making.
