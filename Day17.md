# 🚗 Day 17 – AI Vehicle Cost Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Objective

The goal of this challenge was to analyze a vehicle dataset using Claude AI and generate a responsive HTML dashboard that visualizes operating costs, maintenance expenses, fuel efficiency, environmental impact, and fuel comparisons.

---

# 🛠️ What I Completed

* Downloaded the provided CSV dataset.
* Updated the prompt with my vehicle details.
* Uploaded the dataset into Claude.
* Generated a complete HTML dashboard.
* Tested the dashboard in a web browser.
* Reviewed KPI cards, charts, maintenance analysis, environmental metrics, and E85 calculations.
* Documented the results and learnings.

# 📊 Key Insights from the Dataset

* Different fuel types showed noticeable differences in operating cost per kilometer.
* Maintenance costs varied based on vehicle age, highlighting the long-term impact of ownership.
* Environmental analysis demonstrated that lower emissions do not always correspond to the lowest operating costs.
* The E85 analysis showed that lower fuel prices alone are insufficient for comparison—fuel efficiency and break-even calculations provide a more accurate measure of cost effectiveness.
* Interactive visualizations made it easier to identify cost trends, fuel performance, and environmental impact compared to viewing raw CSV data.

# 💡 Key Learnings

* Learned how AI can automatically transform structured CSV data into a professional dashboard.

* Understood the importance of KPI-driven dashboards for business reporting.
* Learned how visualizations simplify large datasets into actionable insights.

This project demonstrated how AI can transform a structured dataset into an interactive analytical dashboard. By combining prompt engineering, HTML generation, and data visualization, the dashboard converted raw vehicle data into meaningful insights for cost analysis, maintenance planning, fuel comparison, and environmental evaluation.
