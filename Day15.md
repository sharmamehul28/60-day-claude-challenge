# 🔮 Day 15 – AI Generated Vedic Astrology Report

## 📌 Objective

The objective of Day 15 was to use AI to generate a structured Vedic astrology report based on birth details. The report combined astronomical calculations with traditional Vedic astrology principles to provide insights into personality, career, relationships, wealth, current life phase, and future outlook.

---

# 🛠️ Project Overview

Using Claude AI, I generated a comprehensive Vedic astrology report that included:

* Birth Chart Summary
* Planetary Placements
* Personality Analysis
* Career & Wealth Insights
* Relationship Analysis
* Current Vimshottari Dasha
* Five-Year Forecast
* Traditional Remedies
* Overall Self-Reflection

The report also included a reliability assessment, explaining which calculations were based on astronomical data and which sections represented traditional astrological interpretations.
