# 🚀 Day 31 – AI Supply Chain Control Tower
---
### 📖 Overview

Built a complete AI Supply Chain Control Tower, an interactive operations management simulator that places the user in the role of Head of Operations for a global supply chain company.

The application simulates real-world operational disruptions where every decision directly impacts business KPIs, operational efficiency, and overall company performance.

Developed as a single self-contained HTML file using HTML, CSS, and Vanilla JavaScript, with full offline support and no external dependencies.
---
### ✨ Features
-Interactive Supply Chain Control Tower
-Premium Dark Operations Dashboard
-Live Operational Alerts
-3-Minute Real-Time Simulation
-Dynamic KPI Dashboard
-Random Supply Chain Incidents
-Priority-Based Decision Making
-Animated KPI Cards
-Countdown Timer
-Live Event Log
-Delayed Consequence System
-Pause & Resume
-Sound Toggle (UI)
-Help & Instructions Modal
-Performance Grading (A+ to D)
-Play Again Functionality
-Fully Responsive Layout
-Offline Compatible

### 🚨 Operational Alerts
The simulator dynamically generates real-world supply chain disruptions including:

* Port Congestion
* Supplier Delay
* Truck Breakdown
* Warehouse Stockout
-Customs Inspection
-Demand Spike
-Factory Machine Failure
-Weather Disruption

-Damaged Shipment

### 🎮 Gameplay

Players must:

* Monitor incoming alerts
* Prioritize incidents
* Choose the best corrective actions
* Balance operational KPIs
* Handle multiple incidents simultaneously
* Maximize operational performance before time expires
