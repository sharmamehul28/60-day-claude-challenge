# 🔍 Day 13 – AI-Powered Job Discovery with Claude & Indeed Connector

## 📌 Objective

The objective of Day 13 was to use Claude AI with the Indeed Connector to discover job opportunities that match my professional profile and career goals. By combining my resume, job search preferences, and AI-powered analysis, I explored relevant opportunities, identified skill gaps, and gained insights into the current Data Analyst job market.

---

# 🛠️ Project Overview

I created a professional profile, defined my job search criteria, and used the Indeed Connector to search for relevant Data Analyst opportunities. Claude filtered out unrelated roles, ranked the best matches, analyzed the current market, and provided recommendations to improve my chances of securing interviews.

Claude also excluded several roles because they did not align with my preferences.

---

# 📈 Market Insights

The AI-generated market analysis highlighted several important trends.

* Microsoft Excel remains the most requested skill for entry-level analyst roles.
* SQL continues to be one of the most valuable technical skills.
* Power BI and Tableau are becoming important differentiators.
* Python appears frequently in genuine Data Analyst positions.
* Business Analyst roles currently outnumber pure Data Analyst openings for freshers.
* Remote internships are more common than full-time fresher Data Analyst positions.
* Many "Analyst" job titles are actually Operations, Finance, or Sales roles, making it important to carefully read job descriptions.

---

# 🎯 Recommendations

Based on my profile, Claude recommended:

* Learn Power BI and build at least one complete dashboard project.
* Convert GitHub projects into detailed case studies with business insights.
* Apply for both Data Analyst and Business Analyst internship roles.
* Set up job alerts on LinkedIn, Naukri, and Indeed.
* Continue strengthening SQL, Excel, and Python through real-world projects.
