# 🦈 Day 25 – AI Shark Tank Simulator

## 📌 Project Overview

On Day 25 of the ABTalks 60 Days AI Challenge, I built an interactive **AI Shark Tank Simulator** using Claude AI.

The application simulates a real Shark Tank investment pitch where users present their startup idea, answer investor questions, receive business scores, and get funding recommendations.

This project helped me understand how investors evaluate startups based on market opportunity, execution, scalability, profitability, and customer value.

---

# 🎯 Objective

- Build a fully interactive Shark Tank Simulator
- Simulate real investor questioning
- Evaluate startup viability
- Analyze business fundamentals
- Generate investment recommendations
- Practice startup pitching

---

# 🚀 Startup Presented

## Startup Name

**BizPilot AI**

### Problem

Small businesses manage bookings manually, causing operational inefficiencies and revenue loss.

### Solution

An AI-powered platform that combines:

- Smart Booking
- WhatsApp Automation
- AI Assistance
- Invoicing

into one unified system.

### Revenue Model

Monthly SaaS Subscription

### Target Audience

- Salons
- Clinics
- Gyms
- Coaching Institutes

### Funding Ask

₹50 Lakhs for 2% Equity

# 🦈 AI Shark Tank Judges

The simulator included four AI investors:

- Venture Capitalist
- Founder
- Customer
- Angel Investor

Each judge evaluated the startup from a different perspective and asked strategic questions.

---

# 💬 Investor Q&A

The simulation covered:

- Total Addressable Market (TAM)
- Scalability Strategy
- Product Progress
- Execution Risks
- Customer Switching Motivation
- Product Failure Recovery
- Unit Economics (CAC vs LTV)
- Profitability Roadmap
