# 📅 Day 33 – Media Integrity Analyzer

## 📌 Project Overview

On Day 33 of the **ABTalks 60 Days AI Challenge**, I built an interactive **Media Integrity Analyzer** using HTML, CSS, and Vanilla JavaScript.

The application teaches media literacy through hands-on learning by helping users identify misleading headlines, emotional manipulation, and audience-targeting techniques. It provides real-time analysis, interactive challenges, and a final dashboard summarizing the user's learning experience.

- 🎨 Multiple Theme Selection

# 🧠 Challenges Completed

## 📰 Challenge 1 – Headline Detective

### Fictional Headline

> **Local Water Supply 'Poisoned,' Officials Refuse to Explain Shocking Truth**

### What I Identified

- "Poisoned"
- "Officials Refuse to Explain"
- "Shocking Truth"

### Learning

Although the headline sounded alarming, the article explained that it was only a temporary increase in mineral sediment during routine maintenance and that the water remained safe. This demonstrated how sensational wording can create fear without matching the actual facts.

---

## ❤️ Challenge 2 – Emotion Detector

### Fictional Social Post

> **You NEED to see this before it's too late. They don't want you to know how fast this is spreading. Share NOW before it gets taken down.**

### Emotional Response

**Anxious and pressured to share the post immediately without verifying the information.**

### Emotional Triggers Identified

- NEED to see this
- before it's too late
- don't want you to know
- Share NOW
- before it gets taken down

### Learning

This activity demonstrated how urgency, fear, and conspiracy-style language are commonly used to encourage users to react emotionally and share content before verifying its accuracy.
