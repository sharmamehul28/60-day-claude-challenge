# 📦 Day 30 – Supply Chain Builder

## 📖 Project Overview

Today, I built an interactive **Supply Chain Builder** using AI. This simulator is designed to help beginners understand how supply chain decisions impact a business through a hands-on learning experience.

The application starts with a beginner-friendly introduction, generates a random company profile, and guides users through five important supply chain decisions. Every decision updates real-time business metrics, allowing users to see the trade-offs between cost, delivery speed, resilience, customer satisfaction, and sustainability.

---

## 🚀 Features

- Beginner-friendly explanation of Supply Chain Management
- Random company generation for every playthrough
- Interactive decision-making process
- Supplier Strategy Selection
- Factory Location Selection
- Warehouse Strategy Selection
- Transportation Method Selection
- Inventory Strategy Selection
- Real-time business metrics with animated progress bars
- Trade-off explanation after every decision
- Final performance dashboard
- Overall Supply Chain Score (0–100)
- Strengths & Weaknesses Analysis
- Biggest Risk Identification
- Practical Improvement Recommendations
- Replay functionality
- Fully responsive enterprise dashboard
- Dark UI with smooth animations
- Built using React (CDN) in a single HTML file

---

## 🛠 Tech Stack

- HTML5
- CSS3
- JavaScript (ES6)
- React.js (CDN)
- Babel JSX

---

## 📚 Key Learnings

- Learned the fundamentals of Supply Chain Management.
- Understood the impact of supplier selection on business resilience.
- Explored how factory location influences production costs and delivery speed.
- Learned the difference between centralized and distributed warehouse strategies.
- Compared transportation methods such as Road, Rail, Sea, and Air based on cost, speed, and sustainability.
- Understood inventory management strategies and their trade-offs.
- Observed how every business decision affects multiple KPIs in real time.
- Learned how AI can generate complete educational React applications from a single prompt.

---

## 🎯 Outcome

Successfully created and tested an interactive Supply Chain Builder simulator that explains supply chain concepts through real-world business scenarios and live performance metrics.
