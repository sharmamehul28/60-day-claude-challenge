# Day 51 - Product Discovery & Sprint Planning

## Objective

Today I worked on the Product Discovery & Sprint Planning phase of my 10-day capstone project as part of the AB Talks 60-Day Claude AI Challenge.

## Project Chosen

**CareerLaunch AI**

An AI-powered career assistant designed for students and freshers. The platform helps users improve resumes, prepare for interviews, identify skill gaps, generate cover letters, and track job applications from a single dashboard.

## Activities Completed

* Started a dedicated capstone conversation in Claude.
* Completed the project discovery interview.
* Evaluated multiple project ideas.
* Finalized the project scope.
* Defined v1.0 features and exclusions.
* Established Day 10 success criteria.
* Generated a Product Requirements Document (PRD).
* Generated a Days 2-10 Implementation Blueprint.
* Generated a Project Pitch Deck.

## Key Learnings

* Product discovery is critical before development begins.
* Clear scope definition prevents feature creep.
* A strong PRD aligns product goals and development.
* Sprint planning improves execution speed.
* Success criteria should be measurable and realistic.
