# Day 21 – Digital Privacy Dashboard

## 📌 Objective

Built an interactive **Digital Privacy Dashboard** using Claude AI that analyzes a user's reported digital footprint and presents privacy insights through a modern cybersecurity dashboard.

---

## 🛠️ Task Completed

* Read the provided resources.
* Opened Claude and selected an effort level.
* Used the Digital Privacy Dashboard prompt.
* Reviewed the sample dataset.
* Generated the complete interactive HTML dashboard.
* Tested all dashboard interactions in the browser.
* Saved the HTML file.
* Captured dashboard screenshots.
* Uploaded all files to the GitHub repository.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features

* Digital Footprint Score
* Privacy Score
* Exposure Heatmap
* Company Exposure Ranking
* Data Collection Matrix
* Risk Radar
* Digital Twin Profile
* WOW Insights
* Most Valuable Data Assets
* Privacy Improvement Plan
* Privacy Improvement Simulator
* Final Verdict

---

## 🔒 Privacy Insights

* Digital Footprint Score: **58/100 (Moderate)**
* Privacy Score: **44/100 (Fair)**
* Total Services Analyzed: **15**
* Parent Companies Identified: **7**
* Estimated Ecosystem Concentration: **47%**
* Estimated Tracking Surface: **High**
* The dashboard clearly distinguishes **Facts** from **Estimates** to ensure responsible privacy analysis.

---

## ⚠️ Risk Analysis

* Google ecosystem contributes the highest estimated data exposure due to multiple connected services.
* Social media platforms increase behavioral tracking and social graph exposure.
* Shopping and payment services contribute to purchase intent and financial data signals.
* Messaging applications expand communication and relationship mapping estimates.
* Behavioral tracking received the highest estimated risk score among all analyzed categories.
* Privacy Improvement Simulator demonstrates how small configuration changes can improve the overall privacy score.

---

## 💡 Key Learnings

* AI can generate production-quality cybersecurity dashboards from structured prompts.
* Interactive HTML dashboards provide a better user experience than static reports.
* Data visualization makes privacy analytics easier to understand.
* Separating Facts from Estimates improves transparency and trust.
* Effective prompt engineering results in more detailed and accurate AI-generated applications.
* Privacy awareness can be improved through visual analytics and actionable recommendations.
